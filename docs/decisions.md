| 2026-11-19 | Fremdmarkt (geo): a provider page that shows the data run another market's prices | Owner decision: „Umrechnen mit Fußnote“ (convert, with a footnote). Applies ONLY when a provider's page detects the visitor's country and shows the fetch a different market; first case one.com, whose price list shows the GitHub-hosted runners the US display (countryCode US, no VAT). **Data:** the adapter records that market's prices as quoted, in its currency and net (`currency` USD, `vat_included` false, the provider's `vat_rate` 1900), with `foreign_market` {`country`, `currency`, `quote`}: the verbatim heading and table row of the page; source URL and retrieval date are in `evidence`. Only a static fetch as for every adapter: no proxy, no German IP, no cookie or headless tricks. Only USD is converted; a US display in EUR or any other currency, or with a VAT statement, makes the adapter throw (no decision for it). The German display of the same page keeps its own branch and checks unchanged. validate-data: `foreign_market.currency` equals `currency`, not EUR, `vat_included` false. **Conversion:** by the site and the API as for every non-EUR offer: ECB reference rate of the fetch date or the last business day before it (`data/fx/<date>.json`, `fxRateFor`), then German VAT added; rate, date and source are in the API (`fx`, `derived.tco[…].fx`), and `derived.tco[…].foreign_market` names the market. **Footnote** (instead of the generic FX and net-price notes), verbatim: „US-Preis, umgerechnet zum Kurs vom <Datum>, zzgl. 19 % MwSt.; der Preis für Kunden in Deutschland kann abweichen.“ English: “US price, converted at the rate of <date>, plus 19% VAT; the price for customers in Germany may differ.” The note is per VAT mode on the price cells: in net mode it reads „US-Preis, umgerechnet zum Kurs vom <Datum>; der Preis für Kunden in Deutschland kann abweichen.“ (English: “US price, converted at the rate of <date>; the price for customers in Germany may differ.”; net wording approved by the owner on 2026-11-19). **Gate:** the switch between the German display (EUR, gross, no `foreign_market`) and a foreign market display (USD, net, with `foreign_market` whose currency is the offer's) passes the scraper gate in both directions; currency, VAT mode and price checks are skipped only for that switch, unit and term checks stay, the 30 % rule applies within the same market. Any other change of currency, VAT mode or market still blocks. The issue of the data run lists the switch in its own section („Marktwechsel“, „Markt DE -> US: <provider> (<TLDs>)“), the data commit body and with it the data PR name it, so the review sees it. **History:** the same switch starts a new segment in the same history file; nothing is deleted. Entries of a foreign market carry `market`; validate-data checks every segment on its own (currency, unit, VAT mode, market) and the last one against the offer. A currency or VAT change within one market still stops the history. A switch on the same day as the last entry replaces that day's entry, like any second change on one day; the earlier days stay. API v1: `foreign_market` (offer) and `derived.tco[…].foreign_market` are additive. |
